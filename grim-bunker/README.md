# Grim Bunker

A tiny first-person shooter for Brick 1100, raycast in 3D and drawn on the phone's 96 x 65 LCD pixels
with `bridge.lcd`. Fight your way through 3 floors of a monster-filled bunker and reach the exit.

## How to play

- Hold `2` / `8` (or up / down) to walk, `4` / `6` to turn, `1` / `3` to strafe.
- Hold `5` or `OK` to shoot. Ammo is limited, so grab the ammo boxes.
- Hold `0` to see a map of the walls you have seen so far.
- Find the blinking arrow doorway on each floor to go down to the next one.
- `C` pauses the game, where you can restart the floor or quit.

## Monsters and pickups

- **Grunt**: a horned brute that runs at you and bites. 2 hits.
- **Orb**: a floating eye that keeps its distance and spits energy balls. 2 hits.
- **Medkit**: +25 health.
- **Ammo box**: +8 ammo.

The bottom bar shows health, ammo, monsters left and the floor number.
